# Invented equipment demonstration

This example uses a **fictional HF-4500 engine**. All model names, part numbers, measurements and procedures in the sample are invented for demonstrating the app. Never use them for real equipment work.

**Example question:** “What is the fuel pressure at 850 rpm?”

**Expected documented demo value:** 380 bar at 850 rpm, with a reference to the invented service manual's pressure section. The tested LFM demo answered: “The fuel pressure at 850 rpm is specified as 380 bar at that speed.” Actual wording can differ, and a model can give a wrong answer; compare it with the source.

The demonstration shows the intended interaction: choose a database, ask a question, inspect the answer, and check its cited manual information. General chat with the database off is a separate mode.

Only the invented sample may be redistributed under CC0-1.0. No real workshop manual, customer conversation or proprietary database is part of this repository.
